*Last updated: March 12, 2026*

Cereal Syndicate LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the cerealsyndicate.co website and the Podcast Crunch application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Contact Form

When you use the contact form on our website, we collect your name, email address, and message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

### Podcast Crunch

Podcast Crunch collects the minimum information necessary to operate:

- **Account information** — email address and display name used to create and manage your account.
- **Usage data** — podcast subscriptions, favorites, and listening progress, stored to provide a personalized experience.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, or browsing history.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Respond to contact form submissions
- Operate and improve Podcast Crunch
- Provide customer support

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Podcast Crunch is designed for families. Child accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. All child account data is controlled by the parent account holder.

## Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.

Podcast Crunch does not contain advertisements or third-party tracking.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry. Podcast Crunch account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
